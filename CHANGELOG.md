# Changelog

All notable changes to the documents in this repository.

## Terms of Service

### 1.1.0 (2026-10-03)

- Effective date set to October 1, 2026. Status: Final.
- New preamble: names the contracting entity, covers acceptance on behalf of a business, and links the Privacy Policy.
- Describes LotJitsu as cataloging software for sellers; "auction platform" becomes "catalog platform" and "auctions" become "catalogs" throughout.
- Your content: you retain the rights you hold in your uploads; added a note that AI-generated text may not qualify for copyright protection.
- AI-generated content: added a duty to verify identity, maker, age, materials, dimensions, condition, and authenticity before publishing, and a statement that LotJitsu does not authenticate items.
- Plans and payment: cancellation bullet moved up; wording otherwise unchanged.

### 1.0.0 (drafted 2026-08-08)

- Initial draft. Pending legal review; effective date set at launch.
