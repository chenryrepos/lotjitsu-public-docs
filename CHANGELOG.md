# Changelog

All notable changes to the documents in this repository.

## Terms of Service

### 1.2.0 (2026-10-12)

- Effective date set to October 12, 2026. Status: Final. Owner-approved 2026-10-12.
- Plans and payment: plans are billed monthly or yearly; paid plans renew automatically until cancelled; cancellation takes effect at the end of the paid period; no refund of the remainder except where the law requires one. Prepared for the app-store submission, whose reviewers read the Terms against the pricing page.
- Data retention and deletion: account deletion needs no password, can be requested by email or at lotjitsu.com/delete-account when you cannot sign in, and cancels a paid plan immediately.

### 1.1.0 (2026-10-03)

- Effective date set to October 1, 2026. Status: Final.
- New preamble: names the contracting entity, covers acceptance on behalf of a business, and links the Privacy Policy.
- Describes LotJitsu as cataloging software for sellers; "auction platform" becomes "catalog platform" and "auctions" become "catalogs" throughout.
- Your content: you retain the rights you hold in your uploads; added a note that AI-generated text may not qualify for copyright protection.
- AI-generated content: added a duty to verify identity, maker, age, materials, dimensions, condition, and authenticity before publishing, and a statement that LotJitsu does not authenticate items.
- Plans and payment: cancellation bullet moved up; wording otherwise unchanged.

### 1.0.0 (drafted 2026-08-08)

- Initial draft. Pending legal review; effective date set at launch.

## Privacy Policy

### 1.2.0 (2026-10-12)

- Effective date set to October 12, 2026. Status: Final. Owner-approved 2026-10-12.
- What we collect: the phone number's use is stated (support contact about your account); sign-in provider information (Apple, Google), support conversations, the mobile app's camera, photo library and notification-token use, and address lookups.
- Who we share it with: the providers are now named (Amazon Web Services, Stripe, Anthropic, Help Scout, Apple, Google, Cloudflare, Google Analytics) with what each receives. Required for the App Store privacy label and the Google Play Data safety form, which must match this policy.
- Your rights and choices: deletion is possible without a password, in the app or on the website, and by request at support@lotjitsu.com or lotjitsu.com/delete-account; what deletion removes and what is kept (billing records, Terms acceptances, support conversations) is stated; California, EU and UK rights are named.
- Changes to this policy: links the public documents repository.

### 1.1.0 (2026-10-03)

- Effective date set to October 1, 2026. Status: Final.
- New preamble: names the operating entity and covers both lotjitsu.com and the application.
- "Who can see your auctions" becomes "Who can see your catalogs", and now discloses that authorized LotJitsu personnel and service providers may access catalog content to operate, support, or secure the service.
- Service-provider list folded into a single paragraph; providers unchanged.
- "Auctions" become "catalogs" in the retention section.
