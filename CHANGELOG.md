## Terms of Service

- Plans and payment: the no-commission sentence drops the auction vocabulary ("hammer price", "buyer's premium") for "sale price" and "any premium" (owner, 2026-10-12: the product's vocabulary is catalogs and sellers). Wording only; no change in meaning.

### 1.2.0 (2026-10-12)

- Effective date set to October 12, 2026. Status: Final. Owner-approved 2026-10-12.

Prepared for the app-store submission, whose reviewers read the Terms against the pricing page.
- Data retention and deletion: account deletion needs no password, can be requested by email or at lotjitsu.com/delete-account when you cannot sign in, and cancels a paid plan immediately.

## Privacy Policy

### 1.2.1 (2026-11-14)

- Effective date set to November 14, 2026. Status: Final. Version number and date chosen by the owner, 2026-11-14.
- Cookies and analytics: in the United States, analytics cookies are on and no choice is asked; everywhere else they stay off until accepted (owner, 2026-11-04). Location is told from the IP address, looked up on LotJitsu's own servers, not stored and not shared. The footer link is named as it now reads, "Cookie Policy". What we collect, how we use it and who we share it with say the same.
- Advertising measurement: how we use it, who we share it with, and cookies now describe Google Ads conversion measurement. For people in the United States, a verified sign-up or a new paid plan is reported to Google Ads with the time, the plan amount and a hashed email address, plus the ad's click identifier when they arrived from an ad; that identifier is kept in a cookie for up to 90 days. Google is asked not to personalize ads with any of it. In the phone app, iPhone reports only after Allow on Apple's tracking prompt; on Android the Play Store passes the ad click identifier. Wording owner-approved 2026-11-14; no user notice (owner, 2026-11-14).

### 1.2.0 (2026-10-12)

- Effective date set to October 12, 2026. Status: Final. Owner-approved 2026-10-12.
- What we collect: the phone number's use is stated (support contact about your account); sign-in provider information (Apple, Google), support conversations, the mobile app's camera, photo library and notification-token use, and address lookups.
- Who we share it with: the providers are now named (Amazon Web Services, Stripe, Anthropic, Help Scout, Apple, Google, Cloudflare, Google Analytics) with what each receives. Required for the App Store privacy label and the Google Play Data safety form, which must match this policy.
- Your rights and choices: deletion is possible without a password, in the app or on the website, and by request at support@lotjitsu.com or lotjitsu.com/delete-account; what deletion removes and what is kept (billing records, Terms acceptances, support conversations) is stated; California, EU and UK rights are named.
- Changes to this policy: links the public documents repository.
